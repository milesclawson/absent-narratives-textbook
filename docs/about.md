# About

An 18-week high school course examining how race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, and sexuality have shaped the untold and marginalized narratives of history.

## Audience

This book is written for 10th–12th grade students who are curious about the
stories left out of traditional textbooks, willing to take on tedious,
hands-on primary- and secondary-source research, and self-motivated. It
assumes the civic, geographic, and identity-focused analytical skills built
in 9th grade Human Geography and Ethnic Studies (or AP Human Geography), and
builds on that foundation with deeper historiography and systemic analysis.
Readers should be prepared to examine sensitive topics — race, gender,
sexuality, and systemic injustice — with honesty and empathy.

## How to Use This Book

- Read chapters in order — concepts are introduced in dependency order.
- Use the search bar (top right) to jump to a specific term.
- Try the MicroSims as you encounter them; they are the fastest way to build
  intuition for a new concept.
- Check the [Learning Graph](learning-graph/index.md) when you want to see how
  a concept fits into the larger picture.
