# Foundations of Historical Inquiry

## Summary

This opening chapter builds the analytical toolkit the rest of the course relies on: historiography, primary and secondary source analysis, source bias, and the difference between a dominant narrative and a counter-narrative. Students learn to recognize historical silence and marginalization as patterns, not accidents, and practice the self-inquiry, critical thinking, and empathy that hidden-history research requires. By the end of this chapter, students will be able to explain why some stories are told and others are not, and will have the vocabulary to start asking why.
