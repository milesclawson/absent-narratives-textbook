#### 1. What is social construction, as defined in this chapter?

</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. Option B incorrectly grounds the process in biology, which the chapter explicitly denies for race. Option C wrongly limits construction to written law, and option D confuses it with a separate, later concept.
