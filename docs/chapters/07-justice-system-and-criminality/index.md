# The Justice System and the Construction of Criminality

## Summary

Students examine criminality as a social construction rather than a fixed category, tracing how codes and laws are selectively enforced and how that enforcement produces sentencing disparities, mass incarceration, and the school-to-prison pipeline. The chapter covers racial profiling, implicit bias, and prejudice statistics as evidence, and introduces restorative justice and due process as alternative and corrective frameworks to the patterns students identify.
