# Indigenous Sovereignty and Tribal Relations

## Summary

Building on tribal sovereignty from Chapter 5 and Indigenous displacement from Chapter 4, this chapter deepens the course's treatment of Indigenous history and sovereignty: treaty rights, Minnesota Tribal Nations, the boarding school era and the cultural suppression it produced, and the path from federal recognition to self-determination. Students close by examining contemporary Indigenous voice, repatriation, and Indigenous resistance as living, ongoing processes rather than historical footnotes.
