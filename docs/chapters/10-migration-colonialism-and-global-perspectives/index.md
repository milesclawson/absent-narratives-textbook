# Migration, Colonialism, and Global Perspectives

## Summary

This chapter widens the course's lens beyond the United States to forced migration, displacement, and diaspora as global patterns, and examines colonialism and imperialism as the political-economic forces that produce them. Students study cultural diffusion, U.S. foreign policy, nationalism, and border formation, and connect these global patterns back to the anti-Blackness and racial hierarchy introduced earlier in the course.
