# Civil Rights and Liberation Movements

## Summary

Drawing together government, economic, and justice-system exclusion from earlier chapters, students survey the civil rights, suffrage, labor, disability, and LGBTQ+ liberation movements that responded to them. The chapter examines nonviolent resistance and civil disobedience as social movement strategies, traces the legacy of slavery through Reconstruction and Jim Crow to desegregation, and closes with contemporary activism as a continuation of this history rather than a break from it.
