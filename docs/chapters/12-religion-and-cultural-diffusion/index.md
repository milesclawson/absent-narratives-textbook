# Religion and Cultural Diffusion

## Summary

This chapter examines how major world religions — including Shamanism and Animism, Hinduism, Buddhism, Judaism, Christianity, Islam, and African diasporic religions — develop, spread, and change in response to differing social, historical, and political contexts. Students analyze religious persecution alongside religious pluralism, connecting both back to the cultural diffusion patterns introduced in the migration chapter.
