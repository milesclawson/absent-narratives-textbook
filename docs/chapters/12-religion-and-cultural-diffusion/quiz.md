#### 5. How does the chapter distinguish religious persecution from religious pluralism?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>

</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. Option A denies the explicit distinction the chapter draws. Option C misdefines pluralism as absence rather than protected coexistence. Option D is directly contradicted by the chapter's mention of contemporary discrimination against multiple religious minorities.
