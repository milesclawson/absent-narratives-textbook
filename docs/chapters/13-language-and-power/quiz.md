#### 1. How does this chapter define linguistic suppression?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. The natural, accidental decline of a language over time with no policy involved
2. A synonym for code-switching between two dialects
3. The deliberate prohibition or discouragement of a group's language by a dominant power, typically as part of a broader strategy of cultural suppression

</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. Linguistic suppression is the deliberate prohibition or discouragement of a group's language by a dominant power, exemplified by the boarding school era's explicit, stated policy of punishing Indigenous children for speaking their own languages. Option A wrongly frames suppression as accidental rather than a documented, dated policy. Option B confuses it with a different concept entirely.
