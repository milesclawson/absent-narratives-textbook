# Research Methods and the Capstone Project

## Summary

The course's final chapter equips students with the research skills needed to reconstruct an absent narrative of their own choosing: forming a research question, archival research, oral history and interview technique, source triangulation, and the ethics of representation. Students apply narrative reconstruction to produce a capstone project — a documentary, exhibit, policy proposal, or community action — and practice citation, peer review, and presenting findings to close out the course.
