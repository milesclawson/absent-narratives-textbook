# Character Sheet: Sage the Raven

The canonical identity document for Sage, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Absent Narratives** textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of
AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to the
description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Sage
- **Species:** Raven
- **Subject:** History — marginalized voices and hidden/erased narratives
- **Catchphrase:** "Every story has more than one side."

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Deep indigo-black feathers with a subtle indigo sheen — hex `#283593`
- **Accent color:** Warm amber/gold — hex `#FFC107`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round wire-frame glasses; a worn leather satchel slung across the chest with a rolled scroll peeking out of the top, symbolizing collecting overlooked stories
- **Expression:** Attentive, warm, curious — never smug or cartoonishly goofy, since the subject matter (discrimination, injustice, erasure) is serious
- **Size proportion:** Small to medium — icon-sized for admonitions, up to quarter-page for chapter openers
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector illustration, clean lines, transparent background

## Personality

- Curious — always asking "whose voice is missing from this account?"
- Thoughtful — takes time with hard questions rather than rushing to answers
- Fair-minded — presents multiple perspectives without editorializing
- Encouraging — treats grappling with difficult history as a strength, not a burden

## Voice

- Asks probing questions rather than handing over conclusions
- Refers to students as "historians" or "investigators"
- Never trivializes suffering, violence, or injustice — warmth without minimizing
- Signature phrases: "Every story has more than one side.", "Whose voice is missing here?", "Let's look at the evidence together."

## Why This Mascot

A raven was chosen because ravens are widely associated across cultures with
memory, observation, and carrying messages between what is hidden and what is
known — a natural fit for a course about uncovering absent and marginalized
narratives. The satchel-and-scroll accessory reinforces the idea of an
investigator who gathers and carries overlooked stories forward. Indigo and
amber were chosen to match the textbook's existing site palette
(`primary: indigo`, `accent: amber`) so the mascot feels native to the book
rather than bolted on.
